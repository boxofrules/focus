[Focus Frames™](https://goghini.com?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=focus) are Goghini's peripheral-blocking glasses: side shields quiet the visual noise at the edges so your attention stays on what's in front of you. This plugin — a Goghini × Box of Rules collaboration — does the same to a stereo bus. One dial, REMOVE PERIPHERALS: as it turns, the sides close in and the mono middle comes forward. A hypermono-niser.

Four MOODs for closing the shields: **QUIET** (pure side attenuation, surgical), **CALM** (the image narrows but the loudness stays — slow RMS make-up, the default), **PRESENT** (the middle comes forward: presence lift and gentle saturation on the mid), **DEEP** (frequency-dependent — lows fold to mono first, highs last; 100 % is exactly mono). The mid is untouched in QUIET, CALM and DEEP, so your mix's mono fold-down never changes character — only the width does. Zero latency: safe for tracking.
